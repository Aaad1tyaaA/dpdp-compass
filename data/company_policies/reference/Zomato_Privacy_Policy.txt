# Zomato Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — Food Delivery & Hyperlocal Services)
Entity: Zomato Limited | Sector: Food Delivery, Dining & Hyperlocal Logistics | Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you provide directly to us when using our platform:
- Identity & Contact Data: Name, mobile telephone number, email address, and delivery addresses.
- Geolocation Data: Real-time device precise GPS coordinates to facilitate food delivery, calculate delivery fees, track delivery partners, and verify merchant proximity.
- Transaction & Order Information: Items ordered, restaurant preferences, dietary selections, payment confirmation tokens, invoice details, and tipping data.
- Device & Log Data: IP address, device hardware model, operating system, browser type, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- Audio & Communications: Call recordings and chat logs between customer support, delivery partners, and consumers for order resolution, safety, and training.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing
We process personal data for specific and legitimate commercial purposes:
- Order Fulfilment: Connecting consumers with restaurants and assigning independent delivery partners for doorstep delivery.
- Customer Support: Resolving delivery delays, wrong item disputes, refunds, and order cancellations.
- Fraud Detection & Safety: Detecting unauthorized account access, payment fraud, and fake user reviews.
- Personalized Recommendations: Displaying curated cuisines, trending restaurants, and discounts based on prior ordering habits.

3. Sharing and Disclosure with Third Parties
We do not sell personal data. We disclose personal data under strict contractual confidentiality terms:
- Delivery Partners & Merchants: Sharing customer name, delivery address, and masked phone numbers to fulfill orders.
- Payment Gateways: Processing transactions through RBI-authorized payment aggregators and UPI switches.
- Cloud Infrastructure & Analytics Providers: Hosting data on secure Indian cloud regions (AWS / Azure) and debugging performance.
- Law Enforcement: When required by applicable statutory summons, warrant, or court order in India.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by Indian taxation, accounting, and consumer protection laws (typically up to 5 years for financial transaction records). Where an account is voluntarily closed, personal identifying data is anonymized or purged within 90 days, subject to dispute resolution holds.

5. Security Safeguards
We implement technical and organizational security measures including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive records, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication for administrative portals, and periodic penetration testing.

6. Your Rights and Grievance Redressal
Under applicable data protection regulations (DPDP Act, 2023), you may:
- Request access to your order history and registered account data.
- Request correction of inaccurate profile data.
- Request deletion of your personal account by visiting App Settings -> Privacy -> Delete Account.
- Withdraw consent for marketing notifications anytime via notification toggles.
- Grievance Officer: grievance.officer@zomato.com | Address: Zomato Limited, Gurugram, Haryana, India.
