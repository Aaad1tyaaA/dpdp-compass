# CRED Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — Fintech, Credit Card Management & Rewards)
Entity: Dreamplug Technologies Private Limited (CRED) | Sector: Premium Fintech & Credit Card Management | Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect
- Credit Score & Bureau Data: Credit report data fetched from Experian / CRIF / CIBIL based on explicit user authorization.
- Credit Card Metadata: Masked card numbers, issuing bank names, statement due dates, minimum amount due, and payment settlement records.
- Email & Statement Parsing (Optional): Parsing transaction alerts and monthly credit card e-statements via read-only email authorization (Google OAuth / IMAP) for bill reminders and hidden charge detection.
- Financial & UPI Credentials: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, UPI handles, and auto-pay mandates.
- Device & Security Telemetry: Android/iOS OS integrity, jailbreak/root detection, secure enclave validation, and SIM card binding.

2. Grounds for Processing Personal Information
- Credit Card Bill Management: Generating payment reminders, tracking statement cycles, and processing instant bill clearances via BBPS (Bharat Bill Payment System).
- Credit Products & Lending (CRED Cash / CRED Mint): Credit evaluation with partner NBFCs and banks with user consent.
- Rewards & E-Commerce (CRED Store / CRED Pay): Facilitating coin redemption, voucher dispatch, and merchant checkout.
- Cybersecurity & Fraud Mitigation: Detecting unauthorized device logins, anomalous card spending, and payment tampering.

3. Sharing and Disclosures
- Credit Information Companies (CICs): Authorizing score refresh queries.
- Partner Banks & NBFCs: Disclosing loan eligibility and settlement instructions for credit line drawdowns.
- Payment Aggregators & BBPS Network: Processing bill clearing and UPI payments.
- Regulators: RBI, CERT-In, and judicial agencies under applicable Indian statutes.

4. Data Retention, Minimization and Revocation
Users may revoke email parser access anytime from Settings -> Permissions. Upon account closure, credit card statement caches and email tokens are permanently deleted within 30 days, while financial transaction logs are archived in accordance with RBI and PMLA guidelines for 5 years.

5. Security Architecture
CRED is certified under ISO 27001, ISO 27701 (Privacy Information Management), and PCI-DSS Level 1. All parsed statement data is encrypted using end-to-end asymmetric cryptography.

- Escalation to Data Protection Board of India for statutory rights enforcement.
