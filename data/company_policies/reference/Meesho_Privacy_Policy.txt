# Meesho Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — Social Commerce & Reseller Platform)
Entity: Fashnear Technologies Private Limited (Meesho) | Sector: Social Commerce, Reselling & E-Commerce | Last Updated: 2026

1. Information Collected from Users & Resellers
- User Profile: Full name, mobile number, shipping address, email ID, and language preferences.
- Reseller Business Details: Bank account details for margin payouts, GSTIN, business display name, and referral networks.
- Contact Book Data: Uploaded optionally with explicit consent to help resellers share product catalogs via WhatsApp.
- Order & Browsing History: Items added to cart, shared catalogs, delivery status, and returns history.

2. Grounds & Purpose of Processing
- Connecting buyers, suppliers, and third-party logistics partners to complete cash-on-delivery (COD) and prepaid shipments.
- Facilitating reseller commission settlements and UPI bank transfers.
- Fraud prevention, detecting fake order placements, and monitoring delivery OTP confirmations.
- Regulatory compliance with Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules and Indian taxation laws.

3. Sharing with Third Parties
- Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Couriers (Shadowfax, Delhivery, Xpressbees) for shipment delivery.
- Suppliers: Order SKU details, delivery address, and recipient contact for packaging.
- Payment Intermediaries: For processing online payments and reseller payout transfers.
- Government & Regulatory Authorities upon formal notice.

4. Data Retention and Erasure
Data is stored securely during active platform usage. Tax-related invoices are retained for mandatory statutory periods (up to 7 years). Users can request account deactivation and personal data deletion via in-app support or email.

5. Security Controls & DPDP Compliance
We employ AES-256 data encryption, role-based access restrictions, and strict vendor confidentiality agreements.

6. Grievance Redressal
- Grievance Officer: grievance@meesho.com | Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
