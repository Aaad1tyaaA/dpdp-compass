# Flipkart Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — E-Commerce Marketplace & Logistics)
Entity: Flipkart Internet Private Limited | Sector: Multi-Category E-Commerce Marketplace | Last Updated: 2026

1. Personal Information Collected
Flipkart collects personal information during browsing, checkout, delivery, and seller interaction:
- Account Registration: Name, telephone number, verified email address, alternate contact numbers, and delivery pin codes.
- Financial & Payment Details: Tokenized debit/credit card identifiers, saved UPI IDs, Flipkart Pay Later credit limit status, and SuperCoins balance.
- Shipping & Landmark Data: Detailed residential or commercial shipping addresses, recipient contact, and delivery instruction notes.
- Browsing & Interaction Telemetry: Product search queries, wishlists, viewed items, clickstream logs, device hardware parameters, IP addresses, and cookie identifiers.
- Customer Care Audio & Chat: Voice recordings of customer care calls and automated virtual assistant conversations.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing
- Order Fulfillment & Logistics: Routing parcels through Ekart logistics network, sortation centers, and delivery hubs.
- Returns, Replacements & Warranty: Facilitating reverse logistics pickups and warranty validation with manufacturers.
- Personalized Shopping Experience: Recommending relevant products, tailored category deals, and price drop notifications.
- Loss Prevention & Marketplace Integrity: Detecting bot traffic, fraudulent reviews, unauthorized account takeovers, and payment fraud.
- Legal & Tax Record-keeping: Compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, GST invoice generation, and TDS filings.

3. Sharing with Third Parties
- Sellers & Brand Partners: Sharing delivery address, recipient name, and invoice info to enable shipping and tax invoice generation.
- Logistics Partners (Ekart / Third-Party Couriers): Sharing delivery details and contact phone numbers for doorstep drops.
- Payment Gateways & Banking Partners: Routing payment authorizations and executing refund reversals.
- Group Entities: Synergistic operational sharing with Myntra, Cleartrip, and PhonePe under common privacy standards.
- Regulatory & Law Enforcement: Complying with Section 79 of the IT Act, CERT-In cybersecurity directives, and tax authorities.

4. Retention and Right to Erasure
Transactional and order records are preserved for up to seven (7) years to satisfy Indian corporate, accounting, and tax auditing mandates. Non-transactional profile data, search queries, and marketing preferences may be deleted upon user request via Flipkart App -> Account -> Settings -> Delete Account.

5. Data Security Standards
Flipkart maintains industry-standard security safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption, network firewalls, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) mitigation, database encryption, and regular external security audits.

6. Exercise of Data Principal Rights & Contact
- Grievance Officer: grievance.officer@flipkart.com | Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
- Escalation: Consumers may escalate unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act 2023.
