# Paytm Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — Fintech, Payments & Lending)
Entity: One97 Communications Limited (Paytm) | Sector: Financial Technology, Payments & Credit Distribution | Last Updated: 2026

1. Collection of Personal and Financial Information
Paytm collects information necessary to execute financial services, KYC verification, and merchant settlements:
- KYC Identifiers: PAN (Permanent Account Number), Aadhaar verification metadata (via UIDAI offline XML / masked number), government identity documents, date of birth, and father's/spouse's name.
- Financial & Banking Data: Linked bank account numbers, IFSC codes, UPI handles, transaction ledger entries, credit score reports (from CICs with explicit consent), and loan repayment status.
- Device & Security Telemetry: SIM card binding status, device IMEI / UUID, approximate IP geolocation, Wi-Fi SSID, and installed financial app signatures to prevent UPI skimming and trojan malware.
- Communication Logs: OTP delivery logs, customer care dispute tickets, and chat assistant history.

2. Grounds for Processing
- Execution of Regulated Payments: Processing wallet transfers, FASTag recharges, Soundbox audio settlements, and UPI P2P/P2M transfers under RBI guidelines.
- Credit Appraisal & Co-Lending: Facilitating loan origination through regulated NBFC/bank lending partners upon customer's specific consent.
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Fraud Mitigation: Screening against FIU-IND sanction lists, anomaly detection for high-value suspicious transactions.
- Regulatory Compliance: Mandated retention under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) and RBI Master Directions on KYC.

3. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures
- Regulated Banking Partners & Payment Networks: NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India), sponsor banks, and card networks (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard).
- Credit Information Companies (CICs): CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark for credit score fetching and repayment reporting.
- Co-lending NBFCs: Disclosing loan applicant profiles with affirmative consent.
- Statutory & Law Enforcement Agencies: CERT-In, Enforcement Directorate, GST Intelligence, and law enforcement upon valid legal request.

4. Data Retention and Archival
In accordance with PMLA Rule 3 and RBI directives, KYC documents, account opening records, and financial transaction records are securely retained for a minimum mandatory period of five (5) years following the termination of customer relationship. Non-financial behavioral tracking data is erased within 180 days upon verified account deactivation.

5. Information Security Controls
Paytm operates ISO 27001:2013 and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified data centers in India. End-to-end encryption is enforced for all payment payloads. Access to sensitive data is governed by zero-trust architecture and automated audit logging.

6. Rights of Data Principals & Grievance Mechanism
Users may view their transaction statements, correct contact details via profile settings, and raise dispute claims.
- Grievance Redressal Officer: nodalofficer@paytm.com | Address: One97 Communications Ltd, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.
- Escalation: In case of unaddressed grievances within 30 days, customers may escalate to the Reserve Bank Ombudsman and the Data Protection Board of India.
