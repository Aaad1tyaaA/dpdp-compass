# Blinkit Privacy Policy (Reference Tech Corpus — Quick Commerce & Grocery E-Commerce)
Entity: Blink Commerce Private Limited (Blinkit, a Zomato Group Company) | Sector: 10-Minute Grocery Logistics | Last Updated: 2026

1. Category of Personal Information Collected
Blinkit collects information necessary to execute quick grocery, electronics, and home needs fulfillment:
- Identification Data: Mobile phone number (verified via SMS OTP), name, and email ID.
- Physical Location Data: Continuous foreground location data to determine store feasibility, real-time rider tracking, and ETA estimations.
- Order & Basket Metadata: SKUs purchased, brand preferences, replacement preferences, total bill amount, and delivery tip.
- Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID / IDFA, IP address, device model, operating system, and push notification tokens.

2. Utilization of Data
- Instantaneous Warehouse Picking & Delivery: Assigning orders to nearest micro-warehouses and dispatching fleet riders.
- Order Queries & Resolution: Processing refund requests, returns for damaged products, and customer chat tickets.
- Fraud & Promo Misuse Prevention: Flagging multi-accounting, referral fraud, and fake delivery claims.
- Marketing & Dynamic Offers: Sending personalized replenishment notifications and discount alerts.

3. Sharing and Disclosures
- Parent Company & Group Entities: Shared with Zomato Limited for integrated loyalty, cross-service benefits (e.g., Zomato Gold), and unified security monitoring.
- Delivery Partners: Order manifest, customer delivery address, and temporary masked contact number.
- Payment Intermediaries: Secure transmission of billing total to RBI-authorized payment processors.
- Law Enforcement: Compliance with legal notices, judicial orders, and Indian cybercrime requisitions.

4. Data Retention and Erasure Policy
Data is retained as long as the account remains active and as required by GST, income tax, and e-commerce compliance rules. Upon an account deletion request through the app interface, customer identifiers are purged from operational databases within 30 days.

5. Security Architecture
All data traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.3. Databases are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Strict access control lists and multi-factor authentication are mandatory for all production systems.

6. Rights of Data Principals & Grievance Contact
In compliance with the DPDP Act 2023, data principals have rights of access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal.
- Grievance Officer: grievance.officer@blinkit.com | Address: Blink Commerce Pvt Ltd, Gurugram, Haryana, India.
