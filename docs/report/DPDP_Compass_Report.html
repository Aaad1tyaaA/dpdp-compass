01 · OVERVIEWWhat DPDP Compass does

Snapshot of 30 September 2026. The audit engine was rebuilt on 10 October 2026: the system now uses 83 clause chunks, a 14-obligation checklist and six guardrail checks. For the current design and measured results see project/docs/DOCUMENTATION.md and the paper in project/docs/paper.

DPDP Compass answers questions and audits privacy policies using only the text of the DPDP Act. Every answer cites the exact section or sub-clause it relies on — for example § 8(6).

👤 For citizens

  • Ask about data rights in English, Hindi or Marathi
  • See what an app's privacy policy really says
  • Get a trust score and the gaps that affect you

🏢 For businesses

  • Audit a policy against 11 DPDP obligations
  • Law and policy quoted side by side per finding
  • Generate a compliant draft; export PDF / Word / MD
Core principle — no citation, no claim. The AI model only writes from passages of the law retrieved for the question. If the Act doesn't answer it, the assistant says so rather than guessing.

Checking one obligation

Obligation: Breach notification — § 8(6)
LAW evidenceexact sub-clause retrieved from the Act and quoted
vs
POLICY evidenceclosest paragraph found in the uploaded policy
▼
AI modeGroq classifies using both texts. Policy text is treated strictly as data — instructions inside it are ignored.
Rule-based mode (offline)Keyword rules per obligation + word overlap: ≥ 50% → Satisfied, ≥ 25% → Partial, otherwise Missing.
▼
Finding = status + severity + law quote + policy quote + recommended fix   Satisfied Partial Missing Not applicable
Figure 5 — Per-obligation evaluation in compliance_agent.py.

Scoring

StatusWeightMeaning
Satisfied1.0 (full)Policy clearly commits to the duty
Partial0.5 (half)Mentioned but incomplete or vague
Missing0No matching commitment found
Not applicableexcludedDuty doesn't apply — never counted against the company

Score = (Satisfied + 0.5 × Partial) ÷ applicable obligations × 100 — deterministic and reproducible.

The 7 guardrails (run before anything is shown)

CheckWhat it prevents
Citation existsClaims with no source
High-risk evidenceSerious findings resting on weak evidence
Hallucinated citationSections that don't exist in the Act
Confidence rangeImplausible confidence values
Malformed outputBroken or incomplete AI responses
Prompt injectionHidden instructions inside uploaded documents
Unsupported claimStatements not backed by retrieved text

Outputs

  • Citizen: short answer, what it means, relevant rights, cited sections, app trust score.
  • Business audit: overall score, status per obligation, law vs policy side by side, recommended fixes.
  • Policy draft: a complete privacy policy citing DPDP sections.
  • Downloads: PDF (Hindi/Marathi supported), Word, Markdown.

Known limitations

  • SQLite suits a single server; production needs Postgres.
